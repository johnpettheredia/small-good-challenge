Small, not perfect

Every act is free and easy to adapt. Ask first, respect a no, and choose what feels safe. The streak is a gentle nudge—not a score. This is a practice, not a promise about mental health or a 30-day habit guarantee.

Curious about the research?

Studies of kindness and prosocial actions are promising, but the effects vary. A 2023 review found a growing evidence base and called for more research. Thirty days is a friendly container, not a scientific finish line; habit timing varies, and one missed opportunity did not materially affect habit formation in a real-world study.
